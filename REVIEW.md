## What Was Examined and Decided Was NOT a Defect

1. **Calendar Dates & Shift Times as Strings:** Storing `date` and `joined_on` as `"YYYY-MM-DD"` and `shift_start`/`shift_end` as `"HH:MM"` (24-hour IST). This is intentional and compliant with `DATA_MODEL.md`. Treating calendar dates as instant timestamps would introduce arbitrary time zone shifts and DST ambiguities.
2. **`emp_code` as Primary Business Identifier:** Using customer-generated `emp_code` rather than MongoDB's internal `_id` is specified by the API contract. `_id` is an internal implementation detail and must never appear in API requests or responses.
